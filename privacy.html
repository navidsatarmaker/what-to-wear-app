Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026
🔒 100% Offline Promise: What To Wear is built with a privacy-first architecture. All photo processing, wardrobe tagging, and outfit organization occur 100% on your device. We do not operate external media servers or upload your closet photos to any cloud.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase the What To Wear Android application through our website, payment processing is handled securely by our licensed payment partner, Razorpay. We may receive basic transaction confirmation details such as:

  • Your Contact Information (Name, Email address, or Phone Number) provided during checkout.
  • Transaction reference ID, order amount (₹49), and payment timestamp.

We never see, collect, or store your sensitive financial information, such as UPI PINs, card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords. All payment transactions are encrypted and processed through Razorpay's secure, PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

2. In-App Data & Device Permissions

The "What To Wear" Android application operates entirely offline on your smartphone:

  • Camera & Storage Permission: Used exclusively to photograph and import your personal clothing items to create your digital wardrobe.
  • Zero Cloud Sync: All clothing catalogs, custom palettes, profile spaces, and color-tagging computations remain local to your Android device storage.
  • No Analytics or Ad Trackers: The application does not contain advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or background location trackers.

3. How We Use Transaction Information

Any transaction details received upon purchase are used strictly for:

  • Delivering your digital APK download link immediately upon payment completion.
  • Verifying purchase authenticity if you request developer assistance.
  • Complying with applicable legal, tax, and accounting standards in India.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Information is shared only with certified service providers necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Razorpay: Payment aggregator facilitating UPI/Netbanking processing.
  • Cloudflare R2: Secure digital object storage for serving your temporary download link.

5. Security

We adopt industry-standard security practices, including TLS/HTTPS data encryption across our website and short-lived expiring links for digital asset distribution.
